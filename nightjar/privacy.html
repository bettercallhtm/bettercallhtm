Nightjar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026 · App: Nightjar (com.getdebi.nightjar)

Nightjar is an offline single-player game. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

  • No accounts. The game does not ask you to sign in or create an account.
  • No data collection. The game contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it makes no network requests.
  • Local saves only. Your level progress, grades and settings are stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage. They never leave your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
  • Permissions. Vibration is used for short haptic feedback. The standard internet permission is present only because the game engine's web view requires it; the game does not use the internet.
  • Children. The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
